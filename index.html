About Me

Welcome! I'm Tiffanie Hutcheson, a graduate student in Computer Science at Colorado State University. My research focuses on cybersecurity, privacy, and the human factors that influence technology adoption and security decision-making.

Current Research

My current research explores how rurality influences security and privacy beliefs, behaviors, and technology adoption. I'm interested in understanding how infrastructure, community, and access to technology shape cybersecurity practices.
